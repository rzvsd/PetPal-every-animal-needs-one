# Foster home reviews are participant feedback, not a trust score

Foster home pages show how many placements a home completed, how many reviews it received and the average rating. These numbers stay visible because they help people choose a home, and they come from real arrangements rather than from PetPal's judgement.

A review can only be written by the sender or the recipient of a request whose placement has been completed, one review per placement and reviewer (editing replaces it). The home owner's own reviews are excluded from the average, and reviews without a completed placement are not counted. PetPal does not score, rank, approve or certify homes, and moderators do not adjust ratings; they only remove abusive content, as with any other participant text.

This keeps the product boundary from ADR 0001: the rating describes what other participants reported, not what PetPal vouches for. Copy near these numbers must say so and must not use words such as "verified", "trusted" or "approved". A small number of reviews is easy to skew, so the count is always shown next to the average.
