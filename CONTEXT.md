# PetPal Foster

PetPal connects people and organizations who arrange temporary animal care and keep a shared record of it. Participants make care decisions; PetPal moderates use of the platform.

## Language

**Like**:
One participant's expression of interest in another pet; it does not establish mutual interest.
_Avoid_: Match, when only one participant has acted.

**Mutual match**:
Reciprocal interest confirmed by the matching flow. A social walk request or foster request is not a mutual match.

**Social walk request**:
One first message from one dog owner to another, sent without a mutual match. The chat continues only if the recipient accepts. Limited to dogs, one per owner pair and five new requests per sender per day.

**Basic-details checklist**:
A record of whether basic profile information has been supplied, including explicit Unknown answers. Checks use saved information, including saved photos; drafts do not count.
_Avoid_: Trust score, certification, verified profile.

**Platform administrator**:
A person authorized to moderate accounts, reported content and platform access, not to certify animals, foster homes or placements.
_Avoid_: Foster approver, animal reviewer

**Foster home**:
A place offering temporary animal care, managed by one responsible person with an optional team.
_Avoid_: Verified home, accredited home

**Declared capacity**:
The number of animals a home says it can accommodate; it is not a safety assessment or an approval by PetPal.

**Case**:
A participant's published request to find temporary care for a particular animal.

**Request**:
A proposal connecting one case and one receiving home, initiated either by that home or by the person responsible for the case.

**Placement**:
A recorded reservation or period of temporary care connecting an animal and a home. Acceptance alone does not establish receipt.

**Receipt declaration**:
The receiving foster's statement that the animal has arrived, attributed to that participant and communicated to the sender.
_Avoid_: PetPal-verified handover

**Discrepancy**:
Conflicting participant statements about a placement, retained alongside their clarification without a platform verdict on the animal's location.

**Care journal**:
Participant-authored observations and care actions, not veterinary advice from PetPal.
